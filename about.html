About Me

Professional Summary

I'm a passionate Cybersecurity Engineer and Full-Stack Developer with expertise in creating secure, efficient, and user-friendly web applications. With 3+ years of experience in the tech industry, I specialize in MERN stack development and implementing robust security protocols to protect digital assets.

My Background

I completed my schooling at Lions School, Mirzapur, UP, where I built a strong foundation in science and mathematics, fueling my interest in engineering and technology. I then pursued my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science Engineering with a specialization in Cybersecurity from IIT Kharagpur, graduating in 2022 with honors.

Throughout my academic journey, I engaged in various research projects and internships focused on web application security, network penetration testing, and secure coding practices. These experiences have shaped my problem-solving approach and technical expertise in both development and security domains.
